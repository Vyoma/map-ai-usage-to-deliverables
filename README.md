## What this session covered

Most enterprise AI dashboards count logins. Your CFO needs to count deliveries.

The 24-slide deck walks the gap between **what vendors report** (89% adoption) and **what actually shipped** (29% AI-traced output), then builds a 760-line Python engine that maps that gap to dollars: $3,600/yr recoverable spend and 16.9× ROI on a 28-person team.

Six archetypes. Four bot signals. One architectural rule: **lineage is the anchor**.

## Who this is for

- CTOs and CIOs trying to brief the board on AI adoption with numbers that survive scrutiny
- Engineering leaders who have an AI seat budget and zero clarity on whether it's working
- Anyone who has ever seen "89% adoption" on a vendor dashboard and felt vaguely uneasy
